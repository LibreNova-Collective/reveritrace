Privacy policy — draft

Review and publish only after confirming actual practices.

ReveriTrace skills-only distribution processes only the YouTube or YouTube Music history files or recent event records the user provides or authorizes. The packaged local CLI stores history and indexing metadata in a user-selected SQLite database and does not send them to a publisher server. The distributed package contains no publisher backend or telemetry. The local index persists until the user removes it; local SQLite is not encrypted by the application. Users should restrict device access.

When used through ChatGPT or third-party connectors, files and data may be processed and retained by those providers in accordance with their own terms and controls. The developer cannot promise purely on-device processing in those environments. Google controls its authentication and exports. The current package does not collect passwords or OAuth tokens. Do not provide unrelated data.

To revoke a provider connection, use its account settings. To delete local data, remove the relevant DB, auxiliary files and copies. Chat attachments may require separate removal from the host.
